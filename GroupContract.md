Section 1: Terms and Conditions of Group Work
Group Allocation

All group members are expected to contribute equally to the project. Marks will be distributed based on individual contributions as evidenced through GitHub commits, participation in meetings, and task completion. Members who do not contribute fairly may receive lower marks as agreed by the team.

Communication
The team will use Microsoft Teams as the primary communication platform. All members are expected to respond within 24 hours. Weekly meetings will be held to discuss progress, assign tasks, and resolve issues. GitHub Issues will be used for tracking tasks and discussions related to development.

Task Ownership
Tasks will be assigned using GitHub Projects and clearly documented. Each member is responsible for completing their assigned tasks before the agreed deadlines. If a member is unable to complete a task, they must inform the group early so that support or reassignment can occur.

Diverse Working Styles
The team acknowledges that members may have different schedules and commitments. Work will be distributed fairly and deadlines will be set collaboratively to ensure flexibility while maintaining productivity.

Conflict Resolution
Any conflicts will first be discussed within the group in a respectful manner. If a resolution cannot be reached, the issue will be escalated to the lecturer or tutor for further guidance.

Academic Integrity
All work must comply with university academic integrity policies. Any AI-generated code or content must be reviewed, understood, and appropriately acknowledged. All group members must understand the final submission.
